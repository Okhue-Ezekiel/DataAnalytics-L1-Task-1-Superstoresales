# DataAnalytics-L1-Task-1-Superstore Sales Analysis

## Objective
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Superstore Sales data to understand sales, profit, discount vs profit, and regional performance.

## Dataset
- Superstore.csv (9,994 rows) - contains Orders, Sales, Profit, Discount, Category, Region, etc.

## Tools Used
- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib & Seaborn for visualization
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Done
1. Data cleaning and checking nulls/duplicates
2. Sales & Profit trends by Category, Sub-Category, Region, State
3. Discount vs Profit analysis
4. Top 10 most profitable products and loss-making products
5. Monthly sales trend

## Key Insights
- Technology and Office Supplies drive highest profit
- High discounts (>30%) lead to losses
- West and East regions have highest sales
- Furniture has high sales but low profit margin
